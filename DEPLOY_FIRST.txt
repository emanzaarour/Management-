MAPS Management Portal v2.2

1. Upload this complete folder/project to Vercel.
2. Add the existing environment variables:
   GOOGLE_SHEET_ID
   GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_EMAIL
   GOOGLE_PRIVATE_KEY
   SESSION_SECRET
3. Use the current/new active Google private key, never the revoked/exposed old key.
4. Redeploy after adding/changing environment variables.
5. No manual Google Sheet redesign is required for the dashboard analytics update.

New in v2.2:
- MAPS logo in sidebar.
- Analytics Dashboard with month / all months / custom range filters.
- Teacher, program, section and subject analytics.
- Weekly Schedule Excel export for any authorized user.
- Monthly Timesheet Excel export for any authorized user.
